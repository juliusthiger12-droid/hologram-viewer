Hologram Viewer

Plays anything as a Pepper's ghost hologram. Lay your tablet or phone flat under a sheet of clear acrylic at 45° (or in a hologram stand) and look through the sheet.

Your own files

  • Videos and images look best with the subject on a pure black background, because black is invisible in the reflection. Pick several files to make a playlist.
  • 3D models (.glb) are shown in their real colours and slowly spin. Tap "Model" for a wireframe look. In Blender: File → Export → glTF 2.0, format "glTF Binary".
  • Darken background pushes a dark grey video background down to black.

Mirror

The reflection flips the picture. Up–down is right for a tablet lying screen-up under the sheet. If text looks backwards, try the other settings.

Tips

  • Dim the room and turn screen brightness to max.
  • Turn off Auto-Lock so the screen stays on.
  • On iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from there for full screen.
